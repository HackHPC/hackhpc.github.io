For Participants

Get hands-on with real research computing problems

HackHPC events pair you with mentors from national labs, universities, and industry to work on real HPC, AI, and science-gateway challenges — no prior HPC experience required for most events.

What you'll learn

Hands-on exposure to high-performance computing systems, AI/ML tooling, science gateways, and collaborative software development — skills directly transferable to research and industry roles.

Event types

Hackathons and codeathons are short, intensive team challenges (days). Training institutes are longer, skills-focused programs (weeks).

Mentorship

Every team is paired with mentors — see who has mentored past events in the Mentor Gallery.
