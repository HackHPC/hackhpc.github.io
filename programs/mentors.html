For Mentors

Guide the next generation of research computing talent

HackHPC mentors are experienced professionals and academics from national labs, universities, and industry who guide student and faculty teams through hands-on HPC, AI, and science-gateway projects.

What mentors do

Advise a team (or several) through an event — helping scope the problem, unblock technical issues, and prepare a final presentation or deliverable.

Time commitment

Varies by event, from a single day to recurring check-ins across a multi-week faculty institute. Time commitment is listed on each event's site in the Events Archive.
