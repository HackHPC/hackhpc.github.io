About HackHPC

Hands-on collaboration, built into the research computing pipeline

HackHPC traces back to 2018 and has since grown into a recurring network of hackathons, codeathons, training institutes, and faculty development programs — run with partners across national laboratories, research computing centers, and industry.

Mission

Give students, faculty, and researchers hands-on experience with high-performance computing, AI, and science gateways through short, intensive, mentored events — lowering the barrier to entry into research computing careers.

How it works

Each event pairs participants with mentors from partner institutions to tackle a real research-computing challenge over a few days to a few weeks, culminating in team presentations, posters, or working prototypes.

Who's involved

Events are organized in partnership with organizations like ADMI, TACC, ORNL, and the Science Gateways community (SGX3/SGCI) — see the full list on the Partners page.

History

From one hackathon to a recurring program

HackHPC's earliest known events date to 2018 — HackHPC@PEARC18 in Pittsburgh that July, and Cloud HPC Hack @ SC18 in Dallas that November. Since then the program has expanded along several tracks that continue today:

ADMI Hackathons

Annual hackathon at the ADMI Symposium on Computing at Minority Institutions, running since 2022.

FacultyHack@Gateways

Faculty development institute with the Science Gateways Community Institute, running since 2022.

HPC in the City

Hackathon series held alongside the Supercomputing (SC) conference each year since SC20.

SGX3 Training

Codeathons and coding institutes run with the SGX3 Center of Excellence for undergraduate and graduate students.
